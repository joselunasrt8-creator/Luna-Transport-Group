# Scaling Stage: 50–100 Trucks

## Objective
Build a professionally managed carrier with resilient leadership, systems, and capital discipline.

## Required systems
Executive operating plan; functional leaders and succession; budgeting/rolling forecast; fleet replacement/capital plan; customer portfolio strategy; safety management; compliance calendar/independent review; technology/data governance; business continuity/disaster recovery.

## Governance
Owner/board reporting; delegated authority; procurement/payment controls; contract review; insurance/claims oversight; risk register; internal audit; incident escalation; succession.

Analyze contribution and fully allocated profitability by customer, lane, equipment class, and region where relevant. Evaluate return on invested capital and cash conversion—not truck count alone.

Require stable service, safety, cash, data quality, leadership depth, and unit economics before new regions/services/capital commitments.

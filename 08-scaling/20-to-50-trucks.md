# Scaling Stage: 20–50 Trucks

## Objective
Operate through accountable functional managers and consistent controls.

## Required systems
Operations manager; safety/compliance function; maintenance planning/vendor management; sales/account management; finance close/forecast; HR/recruiting; system-based dispatch/asset records; business continuity plan.

## Controls
Role-based access; documented approvals; segregation where practicable; audit trails; periodic internal reviews; incident/corrective-action governance; customer profitability review.

Manage span of control, driver churn, maintenance bottlenecks, insurance cost, customer concentration, receivables, and data consistency.

Require repeatable results across periods/teams before expansion. Add management, maintenance, safety, recruiting, and liquidity capacity ahead of fleet growth.

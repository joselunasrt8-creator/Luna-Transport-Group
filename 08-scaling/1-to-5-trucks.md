# Scaling Stage: 1–5 Trucks

## Objective
Build reliable owner-led operations and prove unit economics.

## Minimum systems
Equipment/driver register; load/customer records; dispatch checklist; maintenance calendar; driver qualification/safety files; accounting reconciliation; cash forecast; incident/exception log.

## Rhythm
Daily: driver/equipment/load readiness and exceptions. Weekly: reconcile miles, revenue, fuel, repairs, collections. Monthly: truck economics and cash runway.

## Gate to add capacity
Document profitable demand, driver coverage, cash/financing, insurance/compliance readiness, maintenance plan, parking, and backup dispatch. Set thresholds from actual baseline.

Risks: owner single point of failure, revenue mistaken for profit, deferred maintenance, no cash forecast, undocumented promises, incomplete load paperwork.

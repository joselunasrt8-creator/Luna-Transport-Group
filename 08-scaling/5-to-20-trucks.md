# Scaling Stage: 5–20 Trucks

## Objective
Move from owner-dependent coordination to role-owned operations.

## Required systems
Dispatch owner and backup; sales/customer owner; fleet maintenance planning; safety/compliance owner; weekly KPI review; standardized onboarding; cash forecast; customer/lane contribution; authority matrix.

Define role cards, handoffs, escalation, approval limits, shift coverage, and backups for dispatch, maintenance, billing, safety.

## Gates
Sustainable dispatcher workload; maintenance turnaround; recruiting/onboarding capacity; working capital; customer concentration visibility; controlled service and safety trends.

Compare each added unit forecast to actual at 30/60/90 days. Pause additions if exception backlog, service failures, cash stress, or control gaps rise.

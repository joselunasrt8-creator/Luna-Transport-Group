# Management Structure

Design roles around recurring decisions and controls. A small company may combine roles, but avoid self-approval for high-risk payments, safety exceptions, or compliance sign-offs where practicable.

Each role card defines mission, responsibilities, permitted/reserved decisions, inputs, outputs, KPIs, backup owner, escalation conditions, and handoffs.

## Cadence
Daily operations huddle; weekly performance review; monthly business review (P&L, cash, customers, safety, people, capital); quarterly strategy/risk review.

Meetings use defined metrics and record decisions, approver, owner, due date, expected result, and review date. Carry overdue actions forward.

Track manager workload, after-hours coverage, open exceptions, decision delays, and single-person dependencies. Add management capacity before service/control quality deteriorates.

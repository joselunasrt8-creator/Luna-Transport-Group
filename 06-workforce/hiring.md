# Hiring

Forecast workforce demand from committed freight, seasonality, turnover, equipment additions, dispatch coverage, and management workload.

## Role profile
Define duties, qualifications, schedule, pay basis, location, equipment/experience requirements, reporting line, safety expectations, and success measures. Review classification and employment requirements with qualified counsel.

## Workflow
Requisition approval → sourcing → consistent screening → interviews → lawful reference/background/qualification checks → conditional offer → onboarding → training → review.

Use job-related, consistently applied criteria. Do not promise freight, earnings, home time, or equipment conditions that cannot be substantiated.

Track source, time-to-fill, recruiting cost, acceptance, onboarding completion, 30/90/180-day retention, and decline/exit reasons. Protect applicant data.

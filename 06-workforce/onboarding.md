# Onboarding

## Before first assignment
Confirm agreements, identity/payroll setup, required qualification status, training, policy acknowledgments, emergency contacts, equipment assignment, access credentials, and required insurance/customer onboarding.

## Driver orientation
Company expectations; safety reporting; dispatch communications; inspection/defect reporting; HOS/ELD where applicable; accident/breakdown response; load documentation; cargo/security; customer conduct; pay/settlement; non-retaliation; escalation contacts.

## Equipment handoff
Record unit condition, inspection, accessories, fuel card, keys, ELD/device, photos as appropriate, open defects, and acknowledgment.

## First 30 days
Check in after first dispatch, first week, and 30 days. Resolve pay, route, equipment, and communication friction. Record training gaps.

Checklist records item, evidence, date, trainer/reviewer, exception, corrective action, and sign-off. Assignment stays blocked if mandatory prerequisites are unmet.

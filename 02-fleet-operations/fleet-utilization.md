# Fleet Utilization

## Equipment states
Use a defined state per unit: available, assigned, loaded/in transit, empty/in transit, at shipper/receiver, planned maintenance, unplanned maintenance, out of service, parked, retired. Record timestamps and reason.

## Measures
- Time utilization = productive dispatched time / available scheduled time (publish denominator).
- Loaded-mile share = loaded miles / total dispatched miles.
- Empty-mile share = empty miles / total dispatched miles.
- Revenue per total mile = load revenue / loaded plus empty miles.
- Downtime rate = unavailable time / scheduled equipment time.
- Contribution per truck-day.

Do not compare measures with different denominators or periods.

## Daily control
Board shows truck, trailer, driver, location, next appointment, load status, HOS feasibility check, maintenance restrictions, next action owner.

## Weekly review
Investigate idle days, empty repositioning, appointment gaps, repair downtime, driver availability, and unprofitable utilization. Reconcile dispatch, ELD/telematics, fuel, odometer, and settlement records. Label estimates.

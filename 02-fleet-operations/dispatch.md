# Dispatch Operations SOP

## Workflow
1. Confirm truck/trailer roadworthiness and restrictions.
2. Confirm driver qualification and availability.
3. Verify HOS feasibility using current authoritative driver/ELD information.
4. Validate commodity, weight/dimensions, equipment, appointments, addresses, contacts, handling, and customer requirements.
5. Confirm rate confirmation and commercial terms.
6. Create unique load record and attach source documents.
7. Send complete instructions and obtain driver acknowledgment.
8. Monitor pickup, transit, appointment changes, detention, breakdowns, and delivery.
9. Capture signed POD/BOL, accessorial proof, timestamps, and communications.
10. Hand off complete billing packet.

## Exceptions
Safety/legal concern: stop affected movement and escalate. Breakdown: protect people/cargo, notify dispatch/customer, arrange approved repair/tow, preserve records. Delay: notify customer and record revised ETA. Never pressure a driver to violate HOS or bypass safety restrictions.

## Required record
Load ID, customer, rate, driver, tractor/trailer, lane, appointment times/time zones, status events, miles, exception log, POD/BOL, accessorial proof, invoice status.

Shift handoff lists open loads, next critical event, risk, customer promise, owner, and deadline.

# Driver Management

## Principles
Safety, lawful operation, clear expectations, timely pay, respectful communication, and consistent treatment outrank dispatch volume.

## Lifecycle
Recruit → screen → verify qualifications → onboard → assign → coach/support → retain or separate. Preserve records under applicable requirements and policy.

## Daily support
Provide dispatch and escalation contacts. Drivers must be able to report fatigue, unsafe conditions, defects, incidents, harassment, and customer conflicts without retaliation.

## Balanced review
Consider safety events, HOS/ELD exceptions, on-time service, preventable damage/claims, inspection/defect reporting, communication, contextualized fuel efficiency, training, and retention. Never incentivize speeding, HOS violations, skipped inspections, or unsafe operation.

## Coaching record
Date, observed fact/source, driver response, context, policy, agreed action, support/training, owner, follow-up. Separate allegations from verified findings. Review pay basis, settlement timing, deductions, and classification with qualified counsel.

# AI Integration

## Suitable uses
Document classification, extraction with source references, summarization, anomaly detection, draft communications, forecast scenarios, SOP search, decision support.

## Boundaries
AI output is a proposal, not company authority. Confidence is not proof. Generated content must not silently become an operational fact, contractual commitment, safety clearance, payment, dispatch instruction, or regulatory determination.

## Output record
Input/source identity; model/version; generated proposal; supporting references; uncertainty/missing fields; validation; human reviewer; approval/decline; final action; timestamp; outcome.

Review source documents and material fields. Incomplete or contradictory evidence routes to exception review, not guessing. High-impact/regulated decisions require designated qualified human.

Do not upload personal driver, customer, financial, or commercially sensitive data to unapproved services. Evaluate field accuracy, unsupported assertions, exception recall, reviewer correction, time saved, and downstream error against manual baseline.

# Revenue Model

## Revenue streams
Identify contract/dedicated, spot/brokered, direct shipper, specialized service (where properly equipped/authorized), fuel surcharge, accessorial, and other revenue separately.

## Load revenue record
Load ID; customer; broker/shipper; lane; dates; equipment; driver; linehaul; fuel surcharge; earned accessorials; deductions/claims; invoice date; due date; cash receipt date; source documents.

## Revenue quality
Track booked, earned, invoiced, and collected revenue separately, plus aging, deductions, claim exposure, customer concentration, and repeatability. Booked rate is not cash; gross revenue is not profit.

## Customer economics
Calculate revenue, attributable loaded and empty miles, variable costs, accessorial recovery, payment timing, service burden, and contribution margin. Document shared-cost allocation methods.

## Commercial guardrails
Review credit/payment reliability, rate and accessorial terms, freight characteristics, appointment requirements, claims history, insurance requirements, and operational fit before accepting work.

# Growth Strategy

## Governing principle
Add profitable, manageable capacity—not trucks for their own sake.

## Prerequisites
1. Verified demand at rates covering fully loaded cost and target contribution.
2. Qualified, available drivers.
3. Equipment, maintenance capacity, insurance, parking, and operating systems.
4. Working capital for fuel, payroll, repairs, insurance, and receivable timing.
5. Dispatch and management capacity with backup coverage.
6. Safety, compliance, and service controls that remain effective at the proposed size.

## Capacity decision memo
Record expected utilization, rates, loaded/empty miles, driver cost, fuel, maintenance, insurance, financing/lease, permits/tolls, overhead, cash timing, downside case, exit obligations, approver, and review date.

Stress-test lower rates/utilization, fuel increases, repairs, delayed payment, driver vacancy, and downtime. Label assumptions and compare forecasts with actuals.

## Pause conditions
Pause expansion when liquidity, maintenance backlog, driver coverage, service, compliance, or management bandwidth falls outside approved limits. Set thresholds from company evidence.

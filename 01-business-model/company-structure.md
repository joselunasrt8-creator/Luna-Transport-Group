# Company Structure

Define accountability before adding management layers. One person may hold several roles in a small fleet, but each outcome needs one accountable owner and a backup.

| Function | Accountable outcome | Core controls |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / executive | Strategy, capital, risk | Approved plan, authority matrix, monthly review |
| Operations / dispatch | Safe capacity and service | Daily board, load controls, exception escalation |
| Sales / customer management | Profitable repeat freight | Customer qualification, account economics |
| Safety / compliance | Safe and compliant operation | Obligation register, audit calendar, corrective actions |
| Fleet / maintenance | Roadworthy available equipment | Asset register, PM schedule, defect control |
| Finance | Accurate economics and liquidity | Close, reconciliations, AR/AP, cash forecast |
| Workforce | Qualified and supported people | Hiring, qualification, onboarding, coaching |

## Decision rights
Document who may approve rates, accessorial concessions, credit terms, repairs, purchases, hiring, removal from service, incident response, claims, and payments. Set numeric limits only after reviewing actual cash and risk.

## Cadence
Daily operating exceptions; weekly utilization, margin, maintenance, and collections; monthly financial close, customer profitability, safety/compliance, and capacity plan.

## Company-specific setup
Record legal entities, authority, identifiers, insurance, equipment ownership, operating geography, service types, role owners, delegated limits, and professional advisors. Do not infer missing details.

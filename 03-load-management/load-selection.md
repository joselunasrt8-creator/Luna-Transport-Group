# Load Selection

## Decision checklist
- Equipment, commodity, weight, dimensions, securement, and handling fit.
- Driver qualification, availability, and HOS feasibility.
- Pickup/delivery appointment and realistic transit feasibility.
- Rate confirmation and accessorial terms.
- Loaded/empty miles and repositioning.
- Fuel, tolls, lumper, detention, parking, permits, and route costs.
- Customer/broker credit, payment, insurance, and claims risk.
- Next-load positioning and impact on commitments.

Compare contribution per total mile and truck-day alongside service risk and cash timing. A high gross rate may be inferior when deadhead, dwell, unpaid accessorials, or poor repositioning are high.

Reject/escalate unclear freight, unsafe or illegal instructions, incompatible equipment, infeasible HOS/appointments, missing confirmation, or terms below approved floors. Record decision and reason.

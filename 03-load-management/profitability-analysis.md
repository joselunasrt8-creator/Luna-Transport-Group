# Load Profitability Analysis

## Unit
One completed load with unique ID and linked source documents. Include attributable empty repositioning and document shared-mile allocation.

## Revenue
Linehaul + fuel surcharge + earned accessorials − credits/deductions/claims. Distinguish billed from collected.

## Variable costs
Fuel, driver pay/settlement, tolls, lumper, permits, load-specific maintenance/consumables, broker fees, and other directly attributable costs. Preserve source and allocation method.

## Calculations
- Revenue per total mile = load revenue / loaded plus empty miles.
- Load contribution = load revenue − attributable variable costs.
- Contribution per total mile = contribution / total miles.
- Contribution per truck-day = contribution / elapsed truck-days occupied.

Keep fixed costs separate; contribution is not net profit. Reconcile definitions with accounting.

## Review
Compare quoted and actual economics; explain variance from rate, deadhead, fuel, dwell, route, pay, repairs, and accessorial recovery. Do not infer a stable lane rate from one load.

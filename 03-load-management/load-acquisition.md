# Load Acquisition

## Channels
Direct shippers, brokers, contracts/dedicated, referrals, and suitable load marketplaces. Track channel cost, conversion, margin, and payment reliability.

## Prospect qualification
Confirm freight fit, lane frequency, volume/seasonality, equipment and handling, appointments, credit/payment, claims history, insurance/onboarding, and decision-maker.

## Pipeline
Lead → qualified → quote requested → quote submitted → negotiation → awarded → onboarding → first load → repeat / lost. Each opportunity has owner, next action/date, lane/equipment, expected economics, and loss reason.

## Quote discipline
Use current cost model, empty repositioning, deadhead, appointment risk, accessorial terms, tolls, fuel, driver availability, and return-load probability. Approved minimum contribution thresholds must come from company data.

## Weekly review
Qualified pipeline, quote win rate, rate realization, first-load conversion, repeat volume, customer concentration, and contribution—not gross booked revenue alone.

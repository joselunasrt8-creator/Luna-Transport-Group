# Cost per Mile

Publish whether each measure uses loaded miles or total dispatched miles. Fleet economics should expose empty-mile burden; retain both views.

Total cost per mile = included operating costs for the period / total miles for the same assets and period.

Report variable, fixed operating, and fully loaded cost per mile. Categories include fuel/DEF, driver compensation/benefits/settlements, maintenance/tires, insurance, equipment payments or depreciation, permits/registration, tolls, factoring/bank fees, dispatch/admin payroll, software, parking, claims, taxes, and overhead.

Define treatment of owner compensation, interest, depreciation, lease expense, capital expenditure, and one-time costs with the accountant. Avoid double counting principal, depreciation, and lease expense.

Use same date window and vehicle scope for costs and miles. Reconcile fuel cards, odometer/ELD, settlements, repairs, insurance, and ledger. Flag estimates/missing records. Break-even rate depends on cost scope and empty-mile ratio; do not substitute generic industry averages for Luna's measured cost.

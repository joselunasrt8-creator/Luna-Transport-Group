# Financial Dashboard Specification

## Sections
Company P&L/cash; revenue and contribution by customer/lane; truck unit economics; cost per loaded/total mile; AR/AP and cash forecast; capital/debt; data quality/unreconciled items.

## KPI dictionary
Every metric defines purpose, formula, numerator/denominator, units, period, scope, exclusions, sources, refresh, owner, target source, and limitations.

## Initial measures
Gross/earned/invoiced/collected revenue; contribution margin; contribution per total mile; fully loaded cost per mile; loaded/empty miles; utilization; revenue per truck-day; maintenance cost per mile; downtime; on-time service; invoice cycle; DSO; overdue AR; cash low point.

## Data status
Mark verified, estimated, stale, incomplete, or unreconciled. Missing is not zero. Daily operations view, weekly commercial/cash exceptions, monthly reconciled financial dashboard. Retain dated snapshots.

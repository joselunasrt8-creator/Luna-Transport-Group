# Truck Economics

For each truck capture revenue, loaded/empty miles, truck-days, driver cost, fuel, maintenance/tires, tolls, insurance allocation, financing/lease/depreciation treatment, permits, downtime, and overhead allocation.

## Acquisition analysis
Purchase/lease price, down payment, interest/rate, term, residual, warranty, expected mileage, maintenance profile, insurance premium, taxes/fees, replacement cycle, exit cost, utilization, and rate downside.

## Monthly unit review
Revenue per truck and total mile; contribution before fixed equipment cost; fully allocated result; cash debt/lease burden; downtime/repair frequency; age/mileage/resale exposure; driver coverage and service quality.

Use documented safety, reliability, repair trend, residual value, capital need, and opportunity cost for replacement decisions. Attach source quotes, assumptions, scenarios, approval, and post-acquisition review date.

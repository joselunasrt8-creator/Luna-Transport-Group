# Cash Flow Management

## Weekly 13-week forecast
Opening cash + expected receipts + other inflows − payroll/settlements − fuel − maintenance − insurance − debt/lease − taxes − vendors − other outflows = projected ending cash.

Each line records amount, due date, confidence, source, owner, and actual settlement. Separate committed, expected, and uncertain items.

## Receivables
Track load/invoice ID, customer, invoice/due dates, amount, POD completeness, dispute, aging, last contact, next action, expected collection.

## Payables
Track vendor, invoice/due date, amount, criticality, dispute, payment status, and cash impact. Do not defer safety-critical maintenance without an approved safe alternative.

## Controls
Separate payment preparation/approval where practicable; independently verify changed bank instructions; reconcile bank accounts; document owner draws and related-party transactions; maintain tax/insurance reserves with professional advice.

## Weekly review
Compare forecast to actual, explain variance, identify liquidity low point, prioritize collections, and stress-test financing/capital decisions.

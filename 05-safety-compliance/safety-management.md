# Safety Management System

Safety takes priority over dispatch volume, appointment pressure, and revenue. Drivers/staff must be able to report hazards, fatigue, defects, incidents, and unsafe instructions without retaliation.

## Components
Leadership commitment; assigned safety owner; driver qualification/training; vehicle inspection/maintenance; HOS/ELD oversight where applicable; incident/near-miss reporting; claims/corrective actions; customer/site hazard review; periodic trend analysis.

## Incident response
Protect life and scene; contact emergency services where needed; obtain medical help; notify designated contacts; comply with reporting; preserve documents, photos, ELD/telematics, dispatch and witness records; notify insurer/customer as appropriate; avoid speculative fault statements.

## Corrective action
Record event, verified facts, contributing conditions, containment, root-cause method, owner, due date, effectiveness check, and closure approval.

Track leading indicators (training, inspections, defect closure, coaching) and lagging indicators (crashes, injuries, citations, claims, out-of-service events). Interpret with exposure/context; never discourage reporting.

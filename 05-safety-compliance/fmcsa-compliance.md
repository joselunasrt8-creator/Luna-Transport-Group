# Safety and Compliance Management

This is a control framework, not a complete legal checklist. Applicability depends on operation type, interstate/intrastate activity, vehicle/commodity, authority, driver status, and current rules.

## Obligation register
For each requirement record applicability decision, authoritative source/version/date, owner, required evidence, due date/frequency, status, reviewer, retention location, and escalation path.

## Topics to assess with qualified compliance support
Operating authority and registrations; insurance filings; vehicle inspection/maintenance; driver qualification; CDL/medical qualification where applicable; HOS/ELD where applicable; drug/alcohol testing where applicable; accident register/reporting; hazardous materials where applicable; permits/IFTA/IRP where applicable; state/local and customer requirements.

## Control cycle
Determine applicability → assign owner → calendar → collect evidence → review → record completion → investigate gap → corrective action → verify closure.

Reassess when equipment, commodities, lanes, authority, driver model, or geography changes. Safety-critical or potentially disqualifying conditions are escalated; dispatch cannot override restrictions.

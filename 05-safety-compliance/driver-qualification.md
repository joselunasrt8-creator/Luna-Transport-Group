# Driver Qualification Controls

Maintain a restricted-access checklist of records required for the actual operation: application, identity/licensing, medical qualification where applicable, prior-employer inquiries, driving record checks, road test/qualification evidence, training, acknowledgments, and other required items. Confirm exact requirements and retention with current authoritative sources and qualified compliance counsel.

## Lifecycle
Determine applicability → collect securely → verify authenticity/validity → complete required checks → record reviewer/date → resolve gaps before assignment → calendar renewals → restrict access → retain appropriately.

## Status
Pending; verified/current; expiring; expired; exception under review; not qualified for assignment. Pending/expired is not cleared.

Limit access to legitimate business need. Apply secure storage and retention/deletion rules. Never put sensitive driver records in public repository datasets.
